# Store listing draft

**App name:** KosRent

**Short description:** Find homes for rent and connect with owners in Kosamba, Surat.

**Full description:**

KosRent helps you find a house for rent or list your rental home in Kosamba, Surat.

- Browse available 1 BHK, 2 BHK and 3 BHK homes.
- Filter homes by bedrooms and search listing details.
- Sign in with Google to see full home details, save homes and call owners.
- Ask questions through private in-app conversations.
- List your home with rent, deposit, address, locality, furnishing and photos.
- Edit your listing or mark it rented when it is no longer available.
- Report listings and conversations, block chat users and delete your account.

KosRent currently offers free listings and chat. It does not collect rent, security deposits or listing payments. Listing details are supplied by owners and are not verified by KosRent. Always inspect the home and verify ownership and rental terms independently.

KosRent serves Kosamba only. Sign-in, listings and chat require an internet connection. In-app message counts update while the app is open; background push notifications are not provided. The service is intended for adults aged 18 or older.
