**Full description:**

```text
Find a rental home or list your home in Kosamba, Surat with KosRent.

FIND A HOME
• Browse owner-submitted 1 BHK, 2 BHK and 3 BHK rental listings.
• Filter by bedrooms and search listing details.
• Compare monthly rent, deposit, locality, furnishing and property photos.
• Sign in with Google to view full details and the owner's phone number, then call directly.
• Save homes and ask questions through private in-app conversations.

LIST YOUR HOME
• Add rent, deposit, exact property address, locality and contact number.
• Upload up to five property photos.
• Publish, edit, remove your listing or mark the home rented.

SAFETY AND CONTROL
• Report a listing or chat user and block unwanted conversations.
• Delete your KosRent account and associated data from Profile or our website.
• Contact the developer at rathodar110@gmail.com.

KosRent is currently free to browse, list homes and chat. It does not collect house rent, security deposits or listing payments.

Listings are supplied by users and are not verified by KosRent. Inspect the home and independently check ownership, authority and rental terms before making arrangements. Listing phone numbers are shown to signed-in viewers. Private messages are stored on the server and are not end-to-end encrypted.

KosRent currently serves Kosamba only and is intended for adults aged 18 or older. An internet connection is needed for sign-in, listings and chat. Message counts update while the app is open; background push notifications are not available.

Operated by Ved Technologies.
Website: https://kosrent.vedtechnologies.co.in
Support: rathodar110@gmail.com
```
